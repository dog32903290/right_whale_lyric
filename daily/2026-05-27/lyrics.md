### English

The Door Hasn't Found Its Person

The table is still uncleared, the phone shakes first  
The family chat lights up: text when you're home  
The address line stops halfway, the cursor won't move  
At the rented door, the nameplate grows another line  

The read dot burns like a little red stamp  
The language key gets stuck beside the thumb  
The later it gets, the more it looks like losing someone  
The faster the answer, the farther the handle sinks in the wall  

Press reply, delete the address  
Turn the phone face down, the ring keeps going  
The cursor holds still, the handle backs into the wall  
The door hasn't found its person  

Press reply, delete the name  
Put the sentence back in the middle of the hand  
Line after line, the nameplate grows  
The door hasn't found its person
