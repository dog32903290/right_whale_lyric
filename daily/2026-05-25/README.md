## 3. 借位位置

### P1. 聽懂以前先不要簽

- borrowed_position: someone at an institutional meeting who needs the language to slow down before signing, agreeing, or being counted as participating
- what_this_position_knows: the meeting concerns someone they love; the form has consequences; being physically present is not the same as understanding
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether the interpretation is complete, whether the next step has been explained, or whether asking again will be treated as delay
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "Do not move my name forward before the sentence reaches me"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not claim to be the parent, the child, an immigrant family, a disabled child, or an Indigenous/migrant language holder; do not turn language access into a savior posture
- evidence_relation_line: institutional pressure -> meeting/form/tablet voice -> signing or nodding changes access and safety -> wanting help pulls against not understanding the terms -> "Slow it down before my name moves"
- object/action anchor: meeting table, form, signature line, interpretation, communication tablet
- hook pressure: 慢一點交給我
- lyric-writing handoff: start from a meeting table and a pen waiting over the name field; keep the tablet voice as a sound event but do not retell the source family's story

### P2. 家裡快散時誰先留下

- borrowed_position: someone who wants to move out but has become the family conflict buffer
- what_this_position_knows: leaving may protect the self; staying may keep the household from escalating
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether the family will actually collapse if they leave
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "I am not the wall of this house"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not claim a specific eldest-daughter life or assign blame beyond the post
- evidence_relation_line: family conflict -> shared home/doorway -> moving out changes family load -> self-preservation pulls against responsibility -> "If I leave, who hears the next argument?"
- handoff status: not advanced today; needs a second source or careful relation map to avoid exploiting one anonymous post

### P3. 不生小孩不是不愛你

- borrowed_position: someone refusing to convert filial love into a child, house, or future proof
- what_this_position_knows: parents interpret grandchildren as continuity; the speaker has economic and ethical reasons not to have a child
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether parents can hear refusal without hearing rejection
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "I can love you without giving you a grandson"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not own reproductive pressure or turn it into a universal anti-family manifesto
- evidence_relation_line: generational expectation -> hospital/house/grandchild talk -> refusal changes filial relation -> care pulls against self-determination -> "Not this way"
- handoff status: not advanced today; strong future hook but high boundary risk

### P4. 租補看得到卻領不到

- borrowed_position: someone reading a legal relief system while the room itself may disqualify them
- what_this_position_knows: the subsidy exists; the room may not qualify; rent can move faster than help
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether applying or asking will trigger a rent or lease response
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "The rule found me too late"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not reuse the 2026-05-24 tenant voice as if it were a new life
- evidence_relation_line: housing pressure -> subsidy rules/lease -> application changes landlord relation -> relief pulls against housing stability -> "I can see the help, but not stand on it"
- handoff status: not advanced today; too close to the previous day's send-button/key song

### P5. 有工作也離不了家

- borrowed_position: someone whose adult project is delayed because rent turns leaving home into a reversible or impossible action
- what_this_position_knows: income exists; autonomy has a price; sharing or returning home changes dignity and family rhythm
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether leaving now means independence or another dependency
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "A room is not the same as arriving"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not flatten Spain's housing crisis into a single private heartbreak
- evidence_relation_line: rent market -> room/key/search portal -> moving changes family and city relation -> independence pulls against staying housed -> "Even my room is borrowed"
- handoff status: not advanced today; good future Spanish-led candidate

### P6. 想結婚但租不起生活

- borrowed_position: someone viewing apartments and realizing marriage, child, and home have become one unaffordable decision
- what_this_position_knows: the relationship has intention; the room is too small or too expensive; fear can make love feel impractical
- what_this_position_cannot_know: whether delaying the home delays the relationship itself
- what_this_position_cannot_say: "I am not scared of you; I am scared of the rent around us"
- what_right_whale_must_not_claim: do not make the couple a generic low-salary symbol or invent their private outcome
- evidence_relation_line: housing/family pressure -> apartment viewing -> room size changes marriage/child decision -> love pulls against survival math -> "We can enter the room, but not the life"
- handoff status: not advanced today; needs a narrower object than "high rent" to avoid commentary
